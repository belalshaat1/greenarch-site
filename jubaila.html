Istirahat Jubaila

A palm-lined arrival walkway leading to courtyard lounges built around the fire and the pool — a resthouse made for gathering.

Type · ResthouseFocus · Arrival & courtyardStyle · ModernLocation · Riyadh
The brief

An arrival worth walking.

A resthouse compound needed an arrival sequence as memorable as its lounges — a palm-lined walkway between stone-clad units leads to courtyards built around the fire pit and the pool.
