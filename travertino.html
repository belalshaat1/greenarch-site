Al-Rabee Villas

A row of villas clad in warm travertine and black accents, lit to frame each entry after dark.

Type · ResidentialFocus · Villa street facadeStyle · ModernLocation · Riyadh
The brief

One material, a whole street.

A row of neighbouring villas needed a shared material language without losing individual identity — travertine cladding and black accent walls repeat down the street, backlit at the base to mark each entry after dark.

The design

Travertine, shadow and light.

The streetA row of travertine-clad villas, lit low along the base at night.

The entryA single villa entrance, framed in travertine and backlit stone.

The wallA planted travertine wall glowing warm against the night sky.
