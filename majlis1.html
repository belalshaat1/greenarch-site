Salmani Majlis

A reception and majlis where modern comfort meets Najdi craft — layered textiles, wood-slat ceilings and seating made for hosting.

Type · Interior designRooms · Majlis & livingStyle · Salmani · NajdiLocation · Riyadh
The brief

Built for hospitality.

The heart of a Saudi home is where guests are welcomed. We layered generous modular seating, handwoven rugs and Najdi textiles under a warm wood-slat ceiling — a majlis that feels grand yet calm.

The design

Layers of comfort and craft.

The feature wallA plastered media wall with a Najdi motif and a firewood niche.

HandcraftA hand-knotted macramé piece anchors the seating.

The second loungeA softer sitting room, framed by a palm-motif tapestry.

The majlisFloor-level seating, set for Arabic coffee and dates.

Colour & patternA red kilim brings warmth against the calm palette.

Najdi detailA carved plaster frieze runs the length of the seating.
